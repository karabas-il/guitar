Song Chords with Diagrams

Paste a song in the usual “chords above lyrics” format, as found on chord sites. Chord diagrams will appear under every line. Lines like [Chorus]: become section headings. To move part of the song to a new page, click ⤓ to the left of a line or section (or put a line with --- in the text). Clicking a chord diagram switches its fingering throughout the song.
