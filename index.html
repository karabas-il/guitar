Circle of Fifths and Song Chords

Two small tools for people learning to play: see which chords belong together, and print a song with chord diagrams right under the lyrics.

Circle of Fifths

An interactive map of keys: major triads around the circle, relative minors inside, key signatures in the middle.

How to use

  1. Click any segment of the circle to choose a key.
  2. The six chords of that key light up: the selected major, its two neighbours and the three minors below them.
  3. Diagrams of these chords in their most common fingering appear under the circle.

Song Chords with Diagrams

Paste a song in the usual chords-over-lyrics format and chord diagrams appear under every line. The result is easy to print.

How to use

  1. Copy a song from a chord site, with chords above the lyrics, and paste it into the box.
  2. Click “Show”. Lines like [Chorus]: become section headings.
  3. Click a diagram or the ‹ › arrows under it to pick another fingering; it changes throughout the song.
  4. Use ⤓ to the left of a line to start a new page, adjust the print scale and diagram size, then click “Print”.
Open the page →
Everything is saved

The song text, chosen fingerings, page breaks and print settings are kept in your browser.

Printing

Open the page directly in your browser, not in a file preview. If the right edge gets cut off, switch to landscape.

Chord names

A small m means minor (Am), 7 means a seventh chord (E7), # is sharp and b is flat (F#, Bb).

Works in any modern browser, on desktop and on mobile.
